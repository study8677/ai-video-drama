# 运镜、光影、音效与配乐英文词库（MiniMax-H3 速查）

写 `integrated_multimodal_description`、`overall_soundscape` 和 `non_diegetic_music` 时，直接组合以下经过视频模型高响应验证的精准英文表达。

## 二、景别与运镜（Shot Size & Camera Motion）

### 景别（Shot Size）
- 全景建立：`wide establishing shot`
- 中景双人对话（短剧最常用）：`medium two-shot` / `over-the-shoulder medium shot`
- 中近景单人表演：`medium close-up`
- 情绪/震惊/台词特写：`tight close-up` / `extreme close-up on eyes`
- 仰拍显压迫/霸气：`low-angle hero shot`
- 俯拍显孤立/危机：`high-angle shot`

### 运镜（Camera Movement）
- 稳定不动（最保口型与连贯）：`the camera holds steady`
- 缓慢推进（情绪升温/揭晓前）：`the camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed`
- 缓慢拉远（结尾留白/反转定格）：`the camera pulls out slightly`
- 轻微跟拍：`smooth cinematic tracking shot`
- 横移摇镜（引入新出场人物/豪车）：`the camera pans smoothly right/left to reveal...`
- 手持微晃（冲突、打斗、紧张感）：`subtle handheld camera tremor heightening tension`

## 三、光影与氛围（Lighting）

- 黄金夕阳暖光：`warm golden-hour sunset backlight, soft lens flare`
- 都市霓虹夜景：`vibrant neon city night reflections, wet asphalt sheen, cool cyan and magenta rim light`
- 豪宅/总裁办公室：`sleek luxury interior, cool floor-to-ceiling window skyline, crisp architectural rim lighting`
- 阴冷雨夜悬疑：`cold overcast rainy night, flickering streetlight, dramatic shadows across faces`
- 古风烛火暖光：`warm flickering candlelight inside a wooden hall, soft floating dust motes`

---

## 四、人物情绪与微动作（Acting Cues）

- 傲慢嘲讽：`crosses arms, looks down scornfully, smug smirk`
- 冷静松弛：`relaxed half-smile, completely unfazed, calm steady gaze`
- 瞬间僵住震惊：`freezes in utter shock, eyes wide and mouth slightly open, face turning pale`
- 恼羞成怒：`smug smile fading into annoyed disbelief, brows furrowing sharply, clenches jaw`
- 恭敬行礼：`bows deeply and respectfully, gestures politely`
- 霸气转身：`turns smoothly on his heel and walks confidently away`

---

## 五、环境音效与动作拟音（`overall_soundscape`）

- 街头夜市：`Warm evening street market ambience, distant chatter of passersby, faint city traffic`
- 豪车急刹登场：`Crisp tire screech of a luxury sedan braking at the curb, solid car door closing, brisk leather footsteps on pavement`
- 办公室/室内对峙：`Quiet upscale office ambience, faint air-conditioning hum, sharp clink of a porcelain teacup set down`
- 雨夜悬疑：`Steady patter of rain on window glass, distant low rumble of thunder, tense heavy breathing`
- 情绪人声细节：`an audible sharp gasp from the stunned woman` / `a soft mocking scoff` / `quiet rustle of fabric`

---

## 六、背景配乐情绪（`non_diegetic_music`）

- 轻喜/反讽开场：`Light playful staccato strings with a slightly cheeky urban comedy-drama rhythm.`
- 铺垫/反击蓄力：`Subtle rhythmic bass pulse and crisp percussion building anticipation for a dramatic twist.`
- 身份揭晓/高潮爆发：`Dramatic cinematic orchestral swell with deep booming bass hit and sweeping strings signaling an epic reveal.`
- 爽感打脸收尾：`Triumphant, stylish modern Chinese short-drama electro-orchestral finale with crisp electric guitar riff and satisfying resolution.`
- 悬疑诡谲留钩：`Eerie low cello drone with sparse high-pitched piano notes and a sudden suspenseful sting at the end.`
- 虐恋/悲情：`Melancholic solo piano and warm expressive erhu/violin melody carrying bittersweet longing.`
